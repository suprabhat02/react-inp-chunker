# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be respectful and assume good faith. Criticise code, not people. Accept
that a maintainer may decline a change for reasons that are about scope or
maintenance cost rather than about your work.

Unacceptable: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages in
this repository, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to Suprabhat Kumar through
[GitHub](https://github.com/suprabhat02) or the contact details on
[suprabhat-dev.com](https://suprabhat-dev.com). Reports are handled
privately.

Responses, depending on severity: a private correction, a public warning,
a temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
